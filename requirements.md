Increase clarity


Create trust


Move the user forward

Checklist:
Do users know what to do in 10 seconds?


Is there a guided action (not just text)?


Can they succeed without reading docs?

Before asking for:
Email


Payment


Data access


Personal info


You must answer:
Why now?


What do they get?


What do they NOT lose?

No data yet


No projects


No messages


No results

 Empty should teach, guide, or motivate.

What if they mess up?


What if the API fails?


What if they abandon halfway?


What if they do things out of order?


Good apps feel forgiving, not brittle.

Who can see what?


Who can edit?


Who can break things?


If this isn’t defined early, it becomes a nightmare later.

Where is it created?


How is it updated?


When is it archived?


Can it be deleted?

Complete something


Fail something


Make progress


Go inactive


Examples:
Progress indicators


Notifications


Subtle rewards


“You’re X% done” signals

Activation metric


Engagement metric


Retention metric


Conversion metric

Delete account


Export data


Re-activate later

What will we add later?


What must NOT break?


What should be modular?
